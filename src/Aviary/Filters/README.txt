Aviary's ad blocker reads the two filter lists in this folder.

  easylist.txt     EasyList      ads           https://easylist.to/easylist/easylist.txt
  easyprivacy.txt  EasyPrivacy   trackers      https://easylist.to/easylist/easyprivacy.txt

Both are written and maintained by the EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/) and are
used here unmodified, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) — they are also available under the
GNU General Public License version 3 (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html). Their
own licence page is https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html.

Aviary does not change these files and does not mix them into any list of its own. The
copies here are the ones Aviary shipped with, downloaded on 13 September 2026. "Update
now" in Settings, and the optional weekly refresh, save newer copies in the profile
folder (filters\) instead; Aviary uses whichever copy the list itself says is newer.

Aviary's own short list of tracker hosts is in TrackerBlocker.cs and is separate from
these.
