What 2.4.2 adds

The engine underneath

A voice

The HTML build was silent. Every cue in 2.4.2 is synthesized — shot, kill, build, demolish, upgrade, mortar launch and boom, arc zap, hull hit, the boss alert — over a low ambient pad, all mutable.

One clock

The simulation runs on a fixed 60 Hz step, and waves spawn on that same clock — so Speed ×2 speeds spawns and movement together. The original sped up the enemies but left the spawns on the wall clock.

Real glow

Where the HTML blurred every shape by hand, a Godot WorldEnvironment glow lights the whole deck at once.

A self-test

One command plays three waves and a wave-10 TITAN with no window at all, and checks the towers fire, the aura boosts, the slow slows and the boss arrives at 1,800 HP.

Room to grow

Built to be extended: new towers and enemies, a second map, saves and meta-progression, difficulty modifiers, screen shake on boss hits.

And what it deliberately kept: no save system and no directive tray — exactly as 2.4.1 shipped. The base v2.4 had directives; the EXP branch dropped them, and so does the port.
