What to judge

Aesthetics decide this phase. These are the details that separate the real thing from a Fluent default.

  • Corner radii. Glass capsules are stadiums — radius is exactly half the height. Classic's address field is a 7px rounded rect, never a pill.
  • The edge highlight. The glass rim is a gradient that is bright at two poles and almost invisible at the flanks. A uniform white border is the loudest tell of a fake.
  • Reload's position. In Classic it lives inside the address field at the right, with "Aa" inside at the left. Outside the field is Chrome.
  • The toolbar tint. Switch Page to Dark site and watch every glyph flip to white in one step. That flip is what people recognise as Safari.
  • The caption buttons. 46px wide, full-bleed rectangles, no radius, no gap, no fade. Close hovers #C42B1C, not the Windows 10 red.
  • Content under glass. Scroll the page. Content must pass beneath the floating capsule — that is the theme, not a detail.

Every value here is a token. Colour, radius, blur and spacing all live in one place per theme, so the WinUI 3 build can lift them straight into two resource dictionaries in Phase 3. Nothing is hardcoded in a control.
Not yet real: nothing renders a web page — the "sites" are hand-drawn so the tint pipeline has something honest to sample.
